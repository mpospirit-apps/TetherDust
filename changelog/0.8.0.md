# 0.8.0 — 2026-08-29

## Upgrade notes for admins
- No migrations and no new environment variables. Rebuild the frontend image:
  `docker compose build frontend && docker compose up -d frontend`.
- Existing tether graphs are read as they are. Nothing needs regenerating, and
  the graph API and schema are unchanged.

## Changed
- The tether viewer is now an isometric city. Code files are slabs and database
  tables are drums; their symbols and columns are the storeys; the edges between
  them are tethers running between the floors they actually touch. Drag to pan,
  shift-drag or right-drag to turn, scroll to zoom, `[` and `]` to nudge.
- Clicking a building opens it — the storeys lift apart with the ground floor
  staying put — and the tethers separate onto the floors they land on.
- Tethers between the same pair of buildings are drawn as one cord. A graph with
  66 edges between 13 pairs draws 13 cords at rest rather than 66 overlapping
  runs, and unbundles when you open either end.
- Tethers route around the buildings between their endpoints, and go dashed for
  any stretch genuinely hidden behind one.
- Storey names appear as you zoom in; at the fitted view they would render too
  small to read.
- Search matches files, tables, symbols and columns, and dims the city to what
  matched. The relationship filters remain; a cord is hidden only when nothing
  it carries passes.
- The layout sliders and the hover tooltip are gone. Placement is now derived
  from the graph rather than adjusted by hand, opening a building replaces the
  spread control, and the details panel carries what the tooltip did.

## Fixed
- The viewer no longer needs a force simulation to settle before it can be read:
  the same graph always produces the same city, in the same places.
